## What it does

Where FallBrief researches the law, KardV5 **weaponises** it. Every dispute needs three things:
1. Your argument, cleanly framed
2. The adverse party's likely counter
3. Your response to that counter

KardV5 generates all three, grounded in the same weave patterns FallBrief uses for research.

## Handles

- Employment tribunals · unfair dismissal, discrimination, TUPE, wages
- Tax tribunals · HMRC compliance checks, penalty appeals
- Tenancy disputes · s.21 challenges, deposit disputes, disrepair
- Consumer rights disputes · Consumer Rights Act 2015 breaches
- Commercial disputes · unpaid invoices, breach of contract, IP

## Tools registered

- `draft_tribunal_skeleton` — full skeleton argument for a tribunal filing
- `draft_negotiation_letter` — settlement offers, dispute responses, without-prejudice letters
- `counter_argue` — generate adverse party's likely arguments + your counter

## Guardrails

Autonomy dial is intentionally locked to Watch/Suggest for the drafting tools. **A human always reviews before filing.** These tools accelerate paralegal work; they do not replace legal judgment. Research-only framing preserved.
